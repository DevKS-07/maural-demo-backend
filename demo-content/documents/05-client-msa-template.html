Master Services Agreement

Thornbury Energy Group · Standard template, revision F · Approved by counsel 4 February 2026 · Owner: Finance

Internal note — this is a template, not an executed agreement. Bracketed highlights are completed per engagement. Any change to clauses 6 (Savings Guarantee), 9 (Limitation of Liability) or 11 (Data and Confidentiality) requires counsel review before issue. Clauses 6 and 11 are the two most frequently negotiated; see the fallback positions noted in each.

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of [Effective Date] between Thornbury Energy Group, a Washington corporation with offices at [Seattle address] ("Thornbury"), and [Client legal name] ("Client").

1. Structure

This Agreement governs all services Thornbury provides to Client. Individual engagements are described in Statements of Work ("SOW") executed under it. Where a SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls, except where the SOW expressly states the clause it supersedes.

2. Services

Thornbury provides building energy services which may include energy auditing, retrofit engineering and delivery, retro-commissioning, and Monitoring-Based Commissioning ("MBCx"). The specific services, deliverables, schedule and fees for each engagement are set out in the applicable SOW.

3. Client responsibilities

  1. Provide safe and timely access to the premises, plant rooms and building systems during agreed working hours.
  2. Provide at least twenty-four (24) months of utility interval data, or authorise Thornbury to obtain it from the utility.
  3. Provide read access to the building management system historian, and where required for commissioning, supervised write access.
  4. Nominate a single facilities point of contact with authority to approve access and schedule.
  5. Notify Thornbury within thirty (30) days of any change to a commissioned sequence of operations made by Client or by any other contractor.

4. Fees and invoicing

Fees are as stated in the applicable SOW. Unless the SOW states otherwise, Thornbury invoices monthly in arrears against engineering progress. Invoices are payable within thirty (30) ([or negotiated]) days of receipt.

Internal note. Revision F moved the default billing trigger from construction completion to engineering progress. Under the prior default, Thornbury financed client-side construction delays, which was the principal driver of days sales outstanding reaching 68 days in Q3 2026 against a 45-day target. Do not revert this default without Finance approval.

5. Change orders

Any change to scope, schedule or fee is effective only when recorded in a written change order signed by both parties. Thornbury is not obliged to perform changed work before a change order is executed, and any resulting delay is not a Thornbury delay for the purposes of clause 8.

6. Savings guarantee

  1. Where a SOW states a guaranteed savings figure, Thornbury guarantees measured first-year savings against the baseline model countersigned by Client under clause 7.
  2. Savings are measured on a weather-normalised basis against that baseline, using the measurement and verification method stated in the SOW.
  3. If measured first-year savings fall short of the guaranteed figure, Thornbury will, at its election, pay Client the shortfall or continue to work the building at no additional fee until the guaranteed figure is achieved.
  4. The guarantee does not apply where: (a) Client's use of the building changes materially from the baseline assumptions, including occupancy, operating hours or process load; (b) the commissioned sequence of operations is altered without notice under clause 3.5; or (c) maintenance specified in the operations and maintenance schedule is not performed.
  5. Thornbury must notify Client of any circumstance it intends to rely on under clause 6.4 within thirty (30) days of discovering it.

Internal note — fallback position. Clients frequently ask to remove the election in 6.3 and require cash settlement. Acceptable fallback: cash settlement capped at the fees paid under the relevant SOW. Do not accept an uncapped cash guarantee.

7. Baseline

Thornbury will prepare a baseline model in accordance with its MBCx Delivery Standard. Client shall countersign the baseline before retrofit work begins. No savings guarantee is effective until the baseline is countersigned.

8. Schedule and delay

Dates in a SOW are estimates unless expressly stated to be firm. Neither party is liable for delay caused by the other party, by a third-party contractor not under its control, or by an event beyond its reasonable control. Where Client-side delay causes Thornbury personnel to be held available, Thornbury may invoice standby time at the rates in the SOW.

9. Limitation of liability

Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement. Each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement is limited to the fees paid or payable under the applicable SOW in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. These limits do not apply to a party's indemnity obligations under clause 10, to breach of clause 11, or to liability that cannot be limited by law.

10. Indemnity

Each party indemnifies the other against third-party claims for bodily injury, death, or damage to tangible property to the extent caused by its negligence or wilful misconduct.

11. Data and confidentiality

  1. Each party shall keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement.
  2. Building performance data — interval data, trend logs, fault records, baseline models and commissioning records — is Client's confidential information. Thornbury may retain and use it to perform the services, to maintain the building record, and in aggregated and de-identified form for benchmarking.
  3. Thornbury may store Client data with subprocessors providing hosting and knowledge management services, subject to confidentiality obligations no less protective than these.
  4. On termination, Thornbury will return or delete Client's confidential information on written request, except that Thornbury may retain the building record for the period required by its professional insurance and by law.

Internal note — fallback position. Healthcare and higher-education clients routinely strike the benchmarking right in 11.2. It is acceptable to remove it. It is not acceptable to agree to delete the building record on termination — clause 11.4's retention carve-out is required by our professional liability cover.

12. Insurance

Thornbury shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability (errors and omissions), workers' compensation and automobile liability in the amounts stated in the SOW, and will provide certificates on request.

13. Term and termination

This Agreement continues until terminated. Either party may terminate for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice, and for material breach on thirty (30) days' written notice if the breach is not cured. Termination does not affect SOWs in progress unless expressly terminated. Client shall pay for services performed and costs reasonably committed up to termination.

14. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in King County, Washington. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets. This Agreement, together with its SOWs, is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.
